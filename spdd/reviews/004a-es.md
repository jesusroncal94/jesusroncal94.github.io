# Review — 004a Spanish

Operation 8 of [canvas 004a](../canvases/004a-spanish.md). Every string the Spanish site shows,
next to its English source. Jesus reviews each row and writes `ok` in **Status**, or writes
the correction there. Corrections are applied to the **Español** cell and the row is marked
again. Only rows marked `ok` ship.

Rules:
- Neutral Spanish for Spain and Latin America, in the first person, with no *vosotros* and
  no regionalisms.
- **Job titles, technologies and products stay in English.**
- Translation never adds, rounds or softens a claim. Figures keep their value; only the
  number format changes (`45,6 %`, `11.000`). Amounts in dollars stay as `$15`, because they
  have no decimals to reformat.
- Tags such as `(C1)` and `*(approved …)*` in a cell are annotations, not part of the text.

Batches: **1. UI, home and CV** (this file, now) · 2. cases 01–02 · 3. cases 03–04.
