# 003 — Get in touch

**As a** recruiter who liked what they saw
**I want** to contact Jesus or take his CV with me
**So that** I can move him into my process

## Acceptance criteria

- WHEN the visitor is anywhere on the page
  THEN the email action is at most one tap away (sticky nav on desktop, bottom bar on
  mobile)
- WHEN "Download CV" is pressed
  THEN the PDF in the current language downloads directly, without a form
- WHEN "Email me" is pressed
  THEN the address is copied with visible confirmation, and a mailto link is offered
- WHEN the visitor reaches the footer
  THEN LinkedIn, GitHub and email are listed, and the phone number is not

## Definition of done

- Contact actions are tracked as conversion events
