## Personas

- **Recruiter**: screens dozens of profiles a day, often on a phone. Decides in under
  twenty seconds whether to open the CV.
- **Hiring manager**: an engineering lead who wants evidence of judgement, not a list of
  tools. Reads one case study end to end.
- **Technical interviewer**: preparing for a session with Jesus. Wants to probe how he
  thinks and what he built.
